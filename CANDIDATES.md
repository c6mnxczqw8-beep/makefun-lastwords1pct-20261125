# Last words at 1% candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 199 characters: the farewell a phone
says as its battery hits 1 percent.

1. Dimmed bravely: "Battery 1%. Tell them I dimmed bravely
   and never once asked for a charger I didn't--" -- the
   cut-off gag, honest but ancient.
2. Photo upload: "With my last 1% I could have called for
   help, but I spent it finishing your photo upload. You're
   welcome. Remember me at 100%." -- a complete dying act:
   sacrifice, grievance, and a bequest of self-image.
3. Location: "Final location shared. It is the couch. It was
   always the couch." -- domestic and true.
4. Notifications: "I die as I lived: at 1%, mid-notification,
   telling you someone liked something." -- bleakly accurate.
5. Flight mode: "Do not mourn me in airplane mode. I chose
   this. I chose all of this except the brightness." -- the
   brightness detail is good.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. The cut-off gag (candidate 1) is the
expected move; candidate 2 spends the last percent the way
the phone spent its whole life -- on the user's photo upload
-- and dies with the complete emotional range of a long
service relationship: sacrifice, grievance ("You're
welcome"), and one vain bequest: remember me at 100%.
